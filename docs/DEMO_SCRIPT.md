# Accelerator demo script (3–5 minutes)

1. Open the fictional Saudi Digital Horizons organization and point out the DEMO label.
2. Switch Arabic/English and show RTL/LTR persistence.
3. Review the organization profile and sourced applicability rationales.
4. Open a versioned framework and its official-source metadata.
5. Create/open an assessment, record a control response, and explain completeness versus readiness.
6. Upload or link evidence; show tenant ownership, checksum, review state, and canonical-control reuse.
7. Open a gap, assign its persisted remediation action, and show overdue/owner views.
8. Show approved cross-framework coverage; distinguish human-reviewed from AI-suggested mapping.
9. Open the executive dashboard and management-attention items.
10. Export the executive PDF/XLSX/CSV or audit JSON package.

No external provider is required for this flow. Scores and the organization are explicitly fictional and are not regulator-issued conclusions.
